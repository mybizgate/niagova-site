    Before you ask

    Straight answers, up front.

    Exactly what we build for your customers, we practise ourselves.

    Do I pay before I see anything?

    No. We build a tailored demo first, usually within 48 hours. Setup is paid only after you approve it.

    Who owns the website and the domain?

    You do. The domain is registered in your name, the content is yours, and you can export it at any time. We manage hosting under the yearly plan.

    Can it be in Bahasa Melayu, or both languages?

    Yes. Bilingual copy is part of the Business and System plans. Most of our clients run English with Malay for key sections.

    What happens to my WhatsApp?

    It stays exactly as it is. The website writes complete messages for your customers and sends them to your existing number. Nothing to install.

    Can you connect to what I already use?

    Usually. Google Business Profile, Maps, payment links and most booking or accounting tools have integrations. If there's no API, we build a practical workaround.

    How long does a full build take?

    Demo in 48 hours, Starter and Business sites live in one to two weeks after approval, Website + System in three to four. Custom builds are scoped on the call.

    What is included in the yearly plan?

    Hosting, SSL, security updates, backups, content and price edits, and support on WhatsApp during business hours. No hourly bills for small changes.

    Were you MyBizGate?

    Yes. Niagova is the new name for the same team and platform. Existing MyBizGate clients keep their sites, plans and support unchanged.
